## Who we are

boatbossloans.com is operated by BOAT BOSS Enterprises.

## Information we collect

- **Newsletter signups.** If you subscribe, your email address and optional first name are sent to our email provider, which stores them and sends the newsletter. We do not keep a copy in our own database.
- **Analytics.** We use privacy-friendly analytics to count page views and clicks (for example, which pages lead people to apply). It does not use cookies or collect personal information.
- **What we never collect.** We do not ask for Social Security numbers, income, dates of birth, or credit information. When you apply, you do so directly with Vantage Recreational Finance under their privacy policy.

## How we use it

To send the newsletter you asked for and to understand which content is useful.

## Your choices

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You can also contact us to ask that your information be removed from our email provider.
