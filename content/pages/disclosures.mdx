## About BOAT BOSS Loans

<Disclosure />

## BOAT BOSS is not a lender

BOAT BOSS Enterprises does not make loans, take loan applications, or make credit decisions. When you click Apply, you leave this site and go directly to the application of Vantage Recreational Finance, Inc. Vantage and its lending partners handle your application, credit decision, loan documents, and closing.

## How we are paid

BOAT BOSS Enterprises receives compensation from Vantage Recreational Finance for loans referred through this site. That compensation does not change what you are offered by Vantage or its lenders.

## General education only

Content on this site is general education. It is not financial, legal, or tax advice, and it is not an offer or commitment to lend. Every borrower's situation is different. Talk to a qualified professional about your circumstances.

## Information we do not collect

This site never asks for your Social Security number, income, date of birth, or credit information. Those are collected only by Vantage through its secure application.
