## From the F&I desk to your side of the table

Kim Sweers spent years arranging boat loans across the finance and insurance desk, the office where a boat deal turns into paperwork. She has watched thousands of buyers sit down to sign, and she knows which ones walked in prepared.

BOAT BOSS Loans is her way of handing every buyer the knowledge she wished they all had: how lenders think, what questions to ask, and how to avoid the surprises that turn a dream boat into a headache.

## Why she started BOAT BOSS Loans

Most boat buyers only finance a boat a few times in their lives. The people across the desk do it every day. Kim built this site so you can walk in knowing more than the person across from you, then apply with a financing partner she trusts, Vantage Recreational Finance, which works with multiple marine lenders.
