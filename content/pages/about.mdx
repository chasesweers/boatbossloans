## Meet THE BOAT BOSS

Kim Sweers, known as THE BOAT BOSS, is a native Floridian based in Fort Lauderdale. She started her career in corporate America in financial services and information technology, then moved into the boating industry in 2001. She has spent more than two decades helping buyers through every part of a boat purchase, including years arranging boat loans at the F&I desk. She is a licensed Florida yacht broker.

## Awards and recognition

Kim has received some of the boating industry's top honors, including the 2025 Darlene Briggs Marine Woman of the Year Award, the 2023 Mercury Marine International Woman of the Year Award, and the Junior Achievement of South Florida Laureate award.

## Industry leadership and safety advocacy

She serves on the Board of Directors of the Marine Retailers Association of the Americas, where she helped create the Dealership of the Future task force. A committed advocate for safety on the water, she served as Master of Ceremonies for the 2025 International Boating and Water Safety Summit and works closely with the National Safe Boating Council.

## The shows

Through the BOAT BOSS Podcast, Yachting Unplugged, and a social following of more than 530,000, Kim shares straight unplugged conversations about buying, owning, and enjoying a lifestyle on the water.

## Why she started BOAT BOSS Loans

Most boat buyers only finance a boat a few times in their lives. The people across the desk do it every day. Kim has sat on both sides of the finance desk, and she built this site so you can walk into your boat purchase knowing exactly what you're signing, then apply with a financing partner she trusts, Vantage Recreational Finance, which works with multiple marine lenders.
